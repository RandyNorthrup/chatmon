# Where Chatmon's sounds come from

Made by `python art/tools/sound-cues.py build` from `cues.json`: each source file is trimmed, faded, made
mono 44.1 kHz and matched in loudness. Sources are in the owner's sound library, whose own SOURCES.md is
where these origins come from. ElevenLabs Sound Effects made on the owner's paid plan are the owner's to
use commercially and are released here under the project's MIT licence, as is the owner's own synthesized
work.
